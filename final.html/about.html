Your Community Starts Here

Kinnly is a niche group guide, with the sole purpose of helping you find your people. Discover others of like mind, or step out of your comfort zone and discover a new passion. Whichever pursuit you need, Kinnly is here to help you discover Dallas, yourself, and your new family of friends.

About Kinnly

Kinnly was created when the creator, Maziko Bakuwa, realized she lacked community after relocating to Dallas to pursue her bachelors degree. The homesick feeling was unbearable, as finding like minded friends to call family proved difficult with little guidance. Kinnly was envisioned to fill the gap that many who move to Dallas for work, school, or a change of scenery may feel.

Niche communities, like the ones featured in Kinnly's guide, are a fun and proactive way to foster curiousity and friendship, with those with similar goals, values, and visions. Whether you are into run clubs, art spectating, language learning, knitting, swimming, yoga, and more, this digital guide is here to make the search for bonds much more easier by giving you a starting point. Adults of all ages are encouraged to step out of their comfort zone and step into exploration of self and community with Kinnly.

Here at Kinnly, we hope to impact you with our values, as you find those who match your values, by displaying:

Who is Kinnly For?

Kinnly is for you, of course! Whether you are a college student who is rediscovering themselves, a resident of DFW exploring their large community, a creative, professional, or hobbyists looking to network, or a new DFW resident looking to fit in, Kinnly is here to connect you to your new community of friends.
If you know someone that Kinnly's guide may be helpful to, feel free to share this as a resource!

How Kinnly Works

Kinnly was designed to be simplistic, yet impactful by being a one stop shop guide. On our Home Page , you can be sure to find our most popular categories list, to show what interests are trending in the DFW area. On our Groups, you can find an interactive guide with multiple social clubs listed. Each category has featured clubs, community centers, or social groups that are listed. Under each, you have the ability to read what they focus on, to discover if they are the right fit for you. Then, if you want to learn more, or are moved to give it a shot and sign up, you can explore their page by being directed to their website or sign up. If you are not yet sure, and test out the group by visiting an event, you can visit their event calendar that is also offered in the groups card.
If you have any suggestions or questions about the content on the guide, you can contact us by filling out a form on our Contact page, where we will be happy to reply within 1-3 days and help you find your next community, skill, or hobby.
